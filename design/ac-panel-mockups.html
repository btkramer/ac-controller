AC Panel

Mockups of the 4-inch, 480 by 480 panel at real pixel size. This version matches firmware 0.3.0, which is on the panel now. The room pages are live: tap − and + to see the numbers roll and the confirmation pulse, tap a mode to see the colors change, and tap the timer or wind button to open its panel. Screens 7 to 10 are new in this round.

Sync off: three pages

1. Overview

Tap a room to open it. Power switches a unit off, or back on in its last mode. Presets set both rooms at once. Top right shows the next scheduled change once Home Assistant provides it.

2. Front Room, cooling

Drag the dial or tap − and +. Top left sets an off timer; top right opens Airflow. Tap either to open it here.

3. Family Room, heating

Heat, with a 1 hour 23 minute off timer running: the timer button turns amber and counts down.

Sync on: two pages

4. Overview, synced

A Linked badge joins the cards. Either card opens Both Rooms, and power switches both units.

5. Both Rooms

One set of controls for both units. Changes go to the Front Room and Home Assistant copies them to the Family Room within about 3 to 10 seconds.

Idle and panels

6. Ambient screen

After 90 seconds untouched. The first touch only wakes the panel, so a tap in the dark can't change anything. From 10 PM to 6 AM it dims further. The trend line is each room's temperature over the last 6 hours.

7. Airflow

Drops down from the wind button; swipe up, tap ×, or tap outside to close. On Both Rooms it sets both units, because Home Assistant's sync doesn't copy airflow.

8. Off timer

Drops down from the timer button. The timer is kept on the panel and survives a restart.

Settings and problems

9. Settings

Hidden on purpose: press and hold "Climate" on the overview to open it. Also shows connection details for troubleshooting.

10. Offline

If WiFi or Home Assistant drops, the controls dim and stop responding, and the banner says which one is down. Info opens Settings.

  • PresetsSleep sets both rooms to Cool at 76°, Comfort to Cool at 72°, All off turns both off. While synced, a preset goes to the Front Room and Home Assistant copies it.
  • Mode colorsCool runs ice cyan to blue, Heat amber to red-orange, Auto mint to teal, Fan lavender to violet, Dry aqua to blue. Off is grey with no glow.
  • Range and step61 to 88 °F in 1° steps, with tick marks every 5°. Taps settle for 0.7 s on a single room and 1.5 s on Both Rooms before sending, so a run of taps sends one change.
  • SyncOn Both Rooms, changes go to the Front Room unit and Home Assistant copies them across. While the Family Room catches up, the Saved note says so.
  • On the panelThe real screen uses these same fonts converted for the device. Glow and gradients come from pre-rendered images, so they cost little. Motion stays inside the dial and icons, because the panel can't redraw the full screen quickly.
